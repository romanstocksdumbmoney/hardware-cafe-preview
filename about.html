Since the hardware days

A 1907 storefront, rebuilt for lunch

Go back in time at Hardware Café near Lake Gaston. Original wood counters and shelves frame gourmet sandwiches on freshly baked bread, homemade desserts, local coffee, wine, and beer.

Locals know the tool-named sandwiches, the Friday dinner board, and the feel of a real Main Street café — this sample just puts that story on a phone-friendly page.
